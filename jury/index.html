An invitation to join the jury of the GESA Workforce Learning Track - the nonprofit awards program, run by MindCET and Sharptext, that recognizes the startups closing the skills gap.

Curated by Sharptext  ·  with MindCET / GESAwards  ·  Finals at BETT London, early 2027

What the jury does.

A clear, finite set of tasks across a single cycle - you take part in the ones that fit your expertise, interest and availability.

01

Shape the criteria

Help define what a strong workforce-learning initiative looks like, and turn that into a clear, fair rubric.

02

Evaluate applicants

Review and score shortlisted entries against the rubric, focusing on your areas of expertise.

03

Vet the finalists

Take a closer, more considered look at the shortlist before winners are chosen.

04

Mentor winners

Offer winners a chance to engage with an experienced voice — guidance and perspective, not a guaranteed outcome.

05

Join the bootcamp

Take part in optional sessions with finalists, sharing practical know-how.

The panel is chaired by the Track Curator, who safeguards the criteria and the integrity of the process.

The panel

Closing the skills gap takes more than one kind of expert.

The jury is built to reflect the whole market, not a single corner of it — seasoned voices from across the workforce-learning ecosystem.

  • — Employers and corporate L&D leaders — the people who buy and deploy learning at scale.
  • — Investors backing workforce and education ventures.
  • — Learning-platform and skills-technology leaders — engaged where they don’t compete with applicants under review.
  • — Independent analysts and thought leaders who anchor the panel’s credibility.
  • — Mission-driven funders and public-good voices.

Choose your level of involvement.

Your time is the most important resource. Every role is opt-in and adapts to your constraints — lend your name, or go all the way through to mentoring. Mix and adjust at any point in the cycle.

~1 hour

Lend your name

Join the named panel and approve the final selection criteria. Your name and organization appear alongside the other jurors.

+4–6 hours

Evaluate

Score a round of shortlisted applicants against the shared rubric, in your areas of expertise.

+6–10 hours

Shape

Co-create the evaluation rubric and selection criteria, and help vet the finalists.

Variable + optional travel

Engage

Mentor a winner, join the London bootcamp, and attend the BETT finals.

Everything is opt-in — there are no obligations beyond the level you choose. Time estimates are indicative for a single cycle and will be confirmed against the GESA 2026 calendar.

What you receive.

The room
