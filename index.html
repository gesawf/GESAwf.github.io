For Partners

Partner with the track that finds the next wave of workforce learning startups.

Corporate training is a $400 billion global market, and demand is rising fast. By 2030, 59% of the global workforce will need reskilling. At the same time, only 0.5% of global GDP is spent on adult lifelong learning. And the skills gap remains the single biggest barrier to business transformation, cited by 63% of employers.

Sources: WEF Future of Jobs 2025 (39% / 59% / 63%); WEF Reskilling Revolution (0.5% of GDP); Training Industry 2025 (market size).

That gap is creating room for new workforce learning solutions. Startups around the world are building products across skills intelligence, adaptive training, work-integrated learning, simulation, credentialing, and more.

This track is built to find the strongest of them. Partners don’t just lend their name — they shape the criteria, sit on the judging panel, and meet finalists directly.

Why this track exists.

Global, not US-anchored

GESA already runs regional semifinals on six continents. The track has that reach from day one — most workforce-learning competitions don’t.

More than an award

Most awards stop at recognition. This one introduces winners directly to a major employer — because an award on its own doesn’t move a startup forward.

Workforce-specific, not generic EdTech

AI is one driver here, not the whole story. Skills intelligence, simulation, credentialing, and work-integrated learning all sit inside the scope — built to last beyond the current AI hype.

Built with operators, not for them

Partners shape the criteria, sit on the panel, and meet the finalists directly. It’s a working group, not a press release.

For Startups

What we’re looking for.

Startups building scalable solutions across workforce learning and development.

AI-powered reskilling & upskilling platforms

Tools that shorten the time from “skill needed” to “skill proven.”

Personalized & adaptive workplace training

Learning that adjusts to the learner’s role, level, and pace in real time.

Skills intelligence, mapping & verification

Tools to map, track, and verify skills across an organization.

Learning embedded in workflows & daily tools

Training that lives inside the CRM, IDE, or ticketing system — not in a separate LXP.

Simulation, practice & experiential learning

Safe environments where people can practice and build real skills.

New models for corporate learning at scale

Cohort programs, apprenticeships, work-integrated learning that fit how organizations actually buy.

Credentialing & skills-based hiring infrastructure

Proof-of-skill systems that work for employers and learners both.

Solutions may target enterprises, SMBs, or individual workers — what matters is a clear link to how organizations build the skills they now need.

Questions, briefly.

Anything missing? Email gesa@sharptext.org.

Is there an application fee? +
No.
What stage of startup can apply? +
Early-stage and growth-stage, with a product in market or near launch. No geographic restrictions.
What does the enterprise engagement look like? +
Each cycle’s engagement is shaped with that cycle’s partner. Winners are introduced directly to the partner team and the opportunity is co-designed.
How are winners selected? +
An international expert panel evaluates against five criteria: clear pain point, pedagogical approach, user experience, business model, and product viability.
How does GESA differ from HolonIQ, GSV Cup, or HR Tech Pitchfest? +
Three differences. GESA runs regional semifinals on six continents — most workforce-learning competitions don’t. The prize is a direct opportunity to engage with an industry-leading enterprise partner, not recognition alone. And the scope is workforce-learning-specific rather than generic EdTech.
Can investors and VCs become partners? +
Yes. Funds typically engage at Partner or Supporting tier — the value sits in pipeline access and direct introductions to founders the track has already validated.
Who runs the track day-to-day? +
MindCET as organizer, with Sharptext running the track day-to-day.
